# Mandatory final review

Review the actual deliverable for the requested mode before reporting completion. Use [acceptance.md](acceptance.md) for relevant detailed checks. Fix observed problems and recheck affected outputs; retain concise version/environment/evidence notes and state unavailable checks.

## Brief or handoff

Read the brief as a designer/build agent who has not seen the conversation. Can they identify the audience, approved facts, unique page purposes, real copy/CTAs, visual direction, responsive/RTL behavior, data destinations, discoverability and acceptance criteria? Reconcile contradictions with the product definition and reference direction. Templates full of prompts or a list of page names are not a finished brief. Distinguish proposed choices, accepted decisions and unknown facts.

For an external builder, inspect the actual inputs being shared for private data and unsupported claims about tools/imports. Confirm the receiver can access the brief and required assets. A local path in a prompt does not transfer its file. Record handoff status separately from implementation; do not run a paid builder or deploy merely to pass a brief review.

## Built or updated website

- Open actual relevant pages and important states on desktop and narrow screens. Compare the experience with the approved direction: useful hierarchy, distinctive composition, readable copy, purposeful assets and coherent navigation. A green build or screenshot of one hero does not prove the whole website works.
- Follow primary actions to their intended result. For connected forms, verify authorized saved-record evidence; label previews/demos honestly. Check keyboard/focus, long content, RTL/mixed text and relevant failure/retry states. Never use real customer data or send unapproved messages for a test.
- Inspect the rendered content, route/status behavior, page-specific metadata, approved canonicals, public sitemap/indexing rules and applicable JSON-LD. Use visible truthful facts. Valid schema and lab scores do not establish search visibility, field performance or complete accessibility conformance.
- Reconcile changed feature readiness, limits/pricing and impacted pages with the product definition and impact map. Check the final version rather than stale previews. Preserve any agreed separation between public marketing, private app and customer-facing product surfaces.

Open the final deliverable links and explain what is briefed, built, handed off, deployed and actually checked. A material failed acceptance check remains pending; do not quietly mark it passed. Routine review/fixes stay within existing authorization and do not add another approval round.
