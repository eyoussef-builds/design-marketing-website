## Product and marketing website consistency

Adapt paths and merge this section into the project's existing instructions without replacing unrelated rules.

- The approved product definition (for example `project.md`) owns facts, scope, commercial decisions and availability. `marketing-site-brief.md` owns public-site structure, copy/design and acceptance requirements. Existing implementation specifications retain their responsibility.
- When implementing an approved change to features, journeys, limits, pricing or release status, inspect the brief's impact map and actual code for affected marketing pages, FAQs, examples, previews, CTAs and public docs.
- Within authorized accessible repository work, update affected site content, the brief, links, metadata, JSON-LD and relevant checks alongside that change. Avoid unrelated redesign. Record when the site is unaffected.
- Implemented does not automatically mean released to every visitor. Preserve accurate availability and do not invent unresolved prices, terms or promises.
- Recheck affected routes and report actual evidence and gaps. UI alone does not prove a saved submission, completed transaction or search result.
- If another repository or unavailable service prevents the update, name the required follow-up instead of claiming completion. This rule adds no background watcher or schedule.
- This maintenance behavior does not independently authorize publication, spending, new services, messages or broader access. Continue routine edits already covered by the request without redundant approvals.
